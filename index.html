root@lockbyte:~/defender-tips

Defender tips

  • Use a passphrase: 4 or more random words are long and easy to remember.
  • Never reuse one password across multiple accounts.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) on social media and email.
  • Never share your OTP or password with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank or support.
  • Avoid names, birthdays, and phone numbers in your passwords.
  • Use a trusted password manager to store your passwords.
